Executive Summary

Metz/Chef2Home · Health Cloud 2026

Summary

Chef2Home by Metz delivers medically tailored home meals in Ohio, paid for by Medicaid waivers, MyCare plans, Area Agencies on Aging, DODD and county levies. This program adds Health Cloud to the Salesforce org Chef2Home already runs, for one referral-to-cash flow: referral documents arrive by email, fax and portal and become Health Cloud referrals; each client is enrolled in the Care Program for their funding source; every delivery becomes a claim line; MuleSoft sends 837P claims to seven clearinghouses and matches each 835 payment back to the exact delivery. The old billing system's renewal ends 31 March 2027. Two AI-native delivery options, both from a 14 December 2026 kickoff with the same team: Option 1, one release over 17 weeks, live at the end of week 15 (~26 March); Option 2, two releases over 18 weeks, with claims and payments (837/835) live at the end of week 12 (~5 March), so billing starts about three weeks before the old system's renewal ends.

G1007Logical GapGo/no-go criteria and fallback. Billing is to be live on 1/29/27, but the first deliveries start 2/1/27 and the first billing run is the first week of March. The old system can run until 3/31/27. (a) What are the go/no-go criteria for 1/29, and when is the last go/no-go date? (b) If one clearinghouse or payer isn't certified by go-live, does its billing stay in the old system until it passes, while the others go live? (c) Is splitting acceptable: intake, client and funding-source go live on 1/29, with the billing go/no-go moving to just before the first March run? (d) If the March run fails, what's the fallback? After 3/31 the old system no longer exists, and nobody has said whether its end date can move.

G0008Missing RequirementData migration: what is the 'old system' whose renewal ends 3/31/27? What record counts are there for active clients, funding sources and authorizations, open referrals, and delivery and claim history? How many years of delivery history are needed?
G0401AmbiguityDirection is 'billing is by delivery: one claim line per delivery.' The questionnaire says some funding sources must be billed by meals per day (1 or 2) instead of 14 meals at delivery, and others need the bill broken out by rules. For a per-day payer, should one delivery become one line with a date span and units = meals, one line per service day (several lines per delivery), or something else? Which payers need which shape, and what do the current saved 837s show?
