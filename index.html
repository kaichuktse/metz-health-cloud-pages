Executive Summary

Metz/Chef2Home · Health Cloud 2026

Summary

Chef2Home by Metz delivers medically tailored home meals in Ohio, paid for by Medicaid waivers, MyCare plans, Area Agencies on Aging, DODD and county levies. This program adds Health Cloud to the Salesforce org Chef2Home already runs, for one referral-to-cash flow: referral documents arrive by email, fax and portal and become Health Cloud referrals; each client is enrolled in the Care Program for their funding source; every delivery becomes a claim line; MuleSoft sends 837P claims to seven clearinghouses and matches each 835 payment back to the exact delivery. The old billing system's renewal ends 31 March 2027. Metz prefers AI-native delivery: 26 weeks, go-live at the end of week 24, conditional on five operating-model commitments.

G1007Logical GapGo/no-go criteria and fallback. Billing is to be live on 1/29/27, but the first deliveries start 2/1/27 and the first billing run is the first week of March. The old system can run until 3/31/27. (a) What are the go/no-go criteria for 1/29, and when is the last go/no-go date? (b) If one clearinghouse or payer isn't certified by go-live, does its billing stay in the old system until it passes, while the others go live? (c) Is splitting acceptable: intake, client and funding-source go live on 1/29, with the billing go/no-go moving to just before the first March run? (d) If the March run fails, what's the fallback? After 3/31 the old system no longer exists, and nobody has said whether its end date can move.
G0001Missing Requirement837 claims: will claims go to a clearinghouse (e.g., Availity, Waystar) or directly to each payer? Which clearinghouse, if any, is already contracted?
G0008Missing RequirementData migration: what is the 'old system' whose renewal ends 3/31/27? What record counts are there for active clients, funding sources and authorizations, open referrals, and delivery and claim history? How many years of delivery history are needed?
G0401AmbiguityDirection is 'billing is by delivery: one claim line per delivery.' The questionnaire says some funding sources must be billed by meals per day (1 or 2) instead of 14 meals at delivery, and others need the bill broken out by rules. For a per-day payer, should one delivery become one line with a date span and units = meals, one line per service day (several lines per delivery), or something else? Which payers need which shape, and what do the current saved 837s show?
G0012Missing RequirementAudit and record locking: at what point do records become non-editable (e.g., after delivery confirmation or claim submission), and which objects? What do annual audit reports need (deliveries, signatures, geotag, timestamps, authorizations)?
G0015Missing RequirementHIPAA and security: does Metz need Shield (encryption and event monitoring), field-level security for protected health information, or a specific audit-trail retention beyond field history?
G0105Missing RequirementHow will environments and releases work? Salesforce PS builds Health Cloud in the new full sandbox while Metz's internal team keeps changing the delivery and scheduling objects in the same org (and possibly the same sandbox). Who owns the deployment pipeline (source control, change sets or CLI-based deploys), the release calendar and sandbox refreshes? When is person accounts turned on in production relative to the deploy?

(b) Do existing records hold several rows per client per funding source (repeat stints), which must be collapsed into one enrollment with N coverage periods? (c) Is the object in production use today, and which flows, reports and scheduling objects reference it?
